{% comment %} HTMX attributes of every control that re-renders the changelist results region (htmx 4). Params: url (the GET URL); cl (ChangeList, from the page context). The server answers with the whole page; the client keeps the results region (hx-select), morphs it in place (a focused sort or page link keeps focus) and refreshes the filters, chips, params and status line out of band.
